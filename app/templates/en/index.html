One cooperative, three crafts

Founded by ten families wanting to make the most of the valley's resources, Tighanimine Cooperative now brings together 34 members around three complementary activities: first-press olive oil, hand-picked and dried aromatic herbs, and traditional wool rug weaving.

Every product is handmade in small batches and sold directly by the cooperative — with no middleman — so that the value stays in the valley.
